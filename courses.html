Public courses

A few times a year I run intensive courses in selected cities across Europe, open to anyone who wants to learn the methodology directly - not just read about it. If you work in fashion, furniture, jewellery, automotive, hospitality, real estate, or any industry where a luxurious approach matters, this is where the ideas turn into practice, alongside a room of people working through the same questions.

We cover how to sell a diamond, a high-end watch, a suite with a panoramic view - and why people buy into luxury in the first place, so you can apply the same principles to make people buy into you.

You don't need to already work in luxury to get value from it - if you want to be perceived as elite and sell at a higher margin, the same principles apply, just adapted to your own industry.

Each course includes

2–3 days of intensive training

Group discussion and hands-on exercises

Personal one-on-one attention within the group
