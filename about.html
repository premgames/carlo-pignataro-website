About

I don't come from luxury.

I had no exposure to it as a child, born to a caring middle-class family in Turin, Italy. Building a career in this industry has been a journey of self-discovery, continuous learning, and hard work.

As an outsider, I needed to acquire the knowledge, the skills, the network, the style, and the language necessary to succeed in a "new world." I've spent the last 15+ years inside the international luxury industry, working in managerial roles with internationally acclaimed brands, consulting some of the most prestigious companies in the world, and entertaining professional and personal relationships with the people who built the houses everyone else studies from a distance.

Today I travel the world - based between Dubai and Dublin - consulting and training organisations who want to align the value they bring to the marketplace with the value people actually perceive. My research into perceived value became the book Sell With Style, with forewords from two people who shaped the industry I learned in.

Luxury can be learned. And luxury sells.

"My job is to help companies and individuals add a touch of luxury and elegance to their business, so they can stay out of price wars, and build strong emotional links with their clients."

- Carlo Pignataro
