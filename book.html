About the author

Carlo Pignataro is an author, entrepreneur, consultant and training specialist best known as "The Luxury Coach." Born in Turin, Italy, and based between Dubai and Dublin, he spent 15+ years inside the international luxury industry - including managerial roles at Gucci and Damiani - before building his own consultancy around one idea: perceived value can be taught.
