For you, personally

One-on-one coaching

I didn't come from luxury - I built my way into it, one relationship and one hard lesson at a time. Managerial roles at Gucci and Damiani, consulting work as an Associate Partner of TJF Group, and personal ties to industry figures like Gianni Bulgari and Giacomo Santucci (both wrote the foreword to my book) taught me one thing above all: luxury can be learned. And luxury sells.

It sells because the world has moved into what authors B. Joseph Pine and James H. Gilmore call "the experience economy" - and luxury is the purest expression of an experience. My own research led me to define luxury as a "celebration of individuality": giving your clients, and yourself, an experience built around what makes you both distinct. That's the lens I coach through - personal, one-on-one guidance built around where you actually are, not a generic playbook.

I'll give you the time and attention to work through what's really in the way, and a clear plan to move your business, your team, or your career forward.

The ideas behind this coaching are the same ones I laid out in full in my book, Sell With Style - with forewords from Gianni Bulgari and Giacomo Santucci themselves.
